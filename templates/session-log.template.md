# Session log

Append-only record of **what each AI session did, what's left open, and what to pick up next**. Newest entry first.

- **Not auto-imported.** Read it when resuming work ("continue", "where were we?") or at the start of a planning session.
- Keep entries to ~4 bullets. Prune quarterly — this is continuity, not an archive.
- Distinct from [`file-log.md`](file-log.md), which records *file changes*. This records *state and intent*.
